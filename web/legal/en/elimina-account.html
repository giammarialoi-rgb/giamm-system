From the app

  1. Open Nurvan and sign in to the account you want to delete.
  2. Go to Settings › Privacy and data (or Profile › Account).
  3. Tap Delete account, type ELIMINA and confirm.

Deletion is immediate and applies to every sign-in method (email and password, Google, Apple). If you had linked Apple, we also revoke the link on Apple's side.

Without the app

Write to from the account's email address (or mention it in your message) with the subject "Account deletion". To protect the account, we may ask you to confirm the request from that address. We delete the account within 30 days of confirmation and let you know when it is done.

Athlete accounts created by a coach

If you sign in with a username and password you received from your coach, the account is managed by them: ask them to remove you, or write to and we will take care of it.

What is deleted

  • Your profile (name, email, password, Google and Apple links).
  • All synced data: programs, workouts, nutrition, supplements, therapies, lab tests, physique checks, photos, settings.
  • For a coach: their athletes, the messages, check-ins, photos and documents exchanged, and the athlete accounts they created.

What remains and for how long

  • The hosting provider's database backups, which are overwritten according to their rotation cycle and are not used for anything else.
  • Documents we are required by law to keep (for example tax documents for a purchase), for the period required by law.
  • The copy of the data on your device: you delete it by uninstalling the app or by clearing the site's data from your browser.
  • A subscription purchased through Google Play or the App Store must also be cancelled from the store's settings, otherwise the store keeps renewing it.
