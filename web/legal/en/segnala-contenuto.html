Anyone can report to us content on Nurvan that they believe to be illegal, even without an account: for example a photo of you uploaded by someone else without your permission, personal data disclosed without consent, threats or a scam. On Nurvan, photos and documents are not public: they stay between a coach and their athletes. Programs are shared with a code and messages are in the chat between coach and athlete.

If you use Nurvan and the problem is in a chat, you can also use Report and Block directly from the chat.

Send the report

Your name and email are needed to confirm receipt and to tell you our decision. Only for reports of child sexual abuse may you leave them blank.

Choose what kind of content you are reporting. Say where the content is (link, user name, chat, date). Explain why the content is illegal. Enter your first and last name. Enter a valid email address. Confirm the good-faith statement. You have sent many reports today. For more, write to info@nurvan.app. We cannot send the report right now. Try again shortly or write to info@nurvan.app. Sending… Report received, number {id}. If you left your email, we have sent you a confirmation: we will write to you with our decision.

What happens next

  • We confirm receipt by email, with the report number.
  • A person reviews it carefully and objectively, normally within 24 hours. We do not use automated systems to decide.
  • If the content is illegal or breaches our terms of service, we remove it or disable access to it and may suspend the account. Whoever uploaded it receives the reasons.
  • We write to you with our decision and the reasons for it. If you disagree, you can contest it by replying to the email within 6 months, or turn to a certified out-of-court dispute settlement body or to the courts.
  • If the report concerns a criminal offence that threatens people's life or safety, we inform the competent authorities.
