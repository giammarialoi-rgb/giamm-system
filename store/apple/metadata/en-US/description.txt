Nurvan is the personal trainer app that tells you what to do in every workout and shows you whether you are improving: training plan, gym log, stats, recovery, nutrition and an AI Coach that actually knows your training.

WHAT CHANGES WITH A PERSONAL TRAINER APP
• You always know what to do: open the app and today's session is there, with sets, reps and rest times
• You never lose your weights again: every set is saved, and next time you see what you lifted
• You see your progress: records, volume and the muscles you trained, week after week
• You decide with numbers: Nurvan tells you when to add weight and when to take a deload week
• You save time: no sheets of paper, phone notes or maths by hand

GYM AND HOME WORKOUTS
• Thousands of ready-made plans by goal, level and the days you have, or build your own
• Import the plan from your personal trainer from a PDF, Excel, Word file or a photo
• Sets, weights, reps and RIR in one tap, with a rest timer and a guided warm-up
• Pilates, mobility, calisthenics and HIIT to do at home, with timed classes
• Plans and race calendar for Hyrox
• Every exercise explained: picture, technique, common mistakes and video

STATS THAT MAKE SENSE
• Volume by muscle group and by period
• An anatomical figure that lights up the muscles you worked (male and female model)
• Estimated one-rep maxes, records and how your weights trend over time

AI COACH
• Reads your gym log and answers with your own numbers, not generic advice
• Helps you choose your progression and handle deload weeks
• Sending data to the AI is optional, needs your consent and you can withdraw it at any time

NUTRITION AND RECOVERY
• Meal plan and food diary with calorie and macro targets, vegetarian or vegan too
• Search foods, scan a barcode or recognise a meal from a photo with the AI
• Recipes and a shopping list
• Sleep, resting heart rate, HRV and steps to estimate your recovery
• Supplements, lab tests and medications in their own sections, with separate privacy controls
• Calculations and advice with the scientific sources cited in the app

FOR PERSONAL TRAINERS AND COACHES
• All your clients in one place: invitations, plans, check-ins and chat
• Calendar, today's priorities and a log of your clients' payments
• Clients join with an invitation and choose what to share

HOW IT WORKS
1. Choose your goal and the days you can train
2. Nurvan suggests a plan, or you import the one from your coach
3. During the session you log sets and weights: at the end you see what improved

PRIVACY
• No ads, no tracking
• Export and delete your data from the app whenever you want
• In 10 languages, with a dark or light theme

SUBSCRIPTIONS
The basics are free. The Standard, Coach and Coach Pro subscriptions unlock more features and renew automatically at the end of each period unless cancelled at least 24 hours before. Payment is charged to your Apple Account; manage or cancel in Settings › your name › Subscriptions. A plan bought on this device also works on your other devices with the same Nurvan account.
