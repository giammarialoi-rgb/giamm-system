## English (secondo localizzazione)

**Name**: `Nurvan`
**Subtitle**: `Workouts and AI Coach`
**Promotional text**:
`Log sets, loads and RIR, see which muscles you train, and ask the AI Coach how you're doing: it answers with your own numbers. For athletes and coaches.`

**Keywords** (≤100):
`gym,workout,training,personal trainer,coach,hypertrophy,weights,recovery,hyrox,pilates,calisthenics`

**Description**:
```
Nurvan is the app for training with a method: program, load log, statistics, recovery and an AI Coach that actually knows your workouts.

TRAINING
• Ready-made programs or programs built by your coach, for the gym, home or Hyrox
• Sets, loads, reps and RIR in one tap, with a rest timer
• Rep suggestions when you add or swap an exercise, always editable
• Every exercise explained: image, execution, common mistakes and video

STATISTICS
• Volume by muscle group and by period
• An anatomical figure that highlights the muscles you trained (male and female model)
• Loads, records and trends over time

AI COACH
• Reads your load log and answers with your numbers
• Helps you decide when to add weight and when to deload
• Sending data to the AI is optional and needs your consent, which you can withdraw any time

RECOVERY AND WELLNESS
• Enter sleep, resting heart rate, HRV and steps by hand: Nurvan estimates your recovery
• Nutrition, supplements, lab results and therapies in their own sections, with separate privacy controls

FOR COACHES
• All your clients in one place: invitations, programs, check-ins and chat
• Calendar, today's priorities and a client ledger
• Clients join by invitation and choose what to share

PRIVACY
• No ads, no tracking
• Export and delete your data from the app whenever you want

SUBSCRIPTIONS
Nurvan is free for the basics. The Standard, Coach and Coach Pro subscriptions unlock more features and renew automatically at the end of each period unless cancelled at least 24 hours before. Payment is charged to your Apple Account; manage or cancel in Settings › your name › Subscriptions. A plan bought on this device also works on your other devices with the same Nurvan account.

Privacy policy: https://nurvan.app/privacy
Terms: https://nurvan.app/termini

Nurvan is not a medical device and does not replace the advice of a doctor or healthcare professional.
```
