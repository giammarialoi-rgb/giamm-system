Your weight before conception may matter for your child. But not every viral detail is real.

A 2024 Nature study found that 2 weeks of a high-fat diet in male mice made about 30% of their sons glucose intolerant, and that 4 weeks back on normal food undid it. In human cohorts, a father with overweight was linked to roughly double the odds of child obesity (an association, not proof).

We checked the original paper: the "3.8× fragment that blocks hexokinase 2" and the "IVF offspring" aren't in it.

What does help before trying for a baby: weight, diet quality, training, less alcohol, no smoking, sleep. Sperm take about two months to form, so start early. A folic acid + zinc supplement tested in 2,370 couples didn't raise live births.

## Alt text (one per slide)

1. A bearded man nuzzles a laughing baby lying on a dark blanket. Headline: "Dad's weight before conception: what's proven". Fact-check, Nature 2024.
2. Text slide: the claim "A father's diet before conception reaches his child." We read the original Nature paper; some of it holds up, some viral details aren't in it.
3. Large number "~30%": of sons became glucose intolerant after their fathers ate a high-fat diet for 2 weeks before mating; their body weight didn't change. Tomar et al., Nature 2024, mice.
4. "It was reversible": fathers who returned to normal food for 4 weeks before mating had normal offspring; the signal came from maturing sperm, not a mutation.
5. Bar chart of child obesity odds by father's weight in families with a normal-weight mother: normal-weight dad 1, dad with overweight 2.26, dad with obesity 6.44. Observational data from Leipzig cohorts, Tomar et al. 2024.
6. Fact-check list: "a fragment rises 3.8× and blocks hexokinase 2" and "the 30% came from IVF" are not in the paper; offspring came from natural mating and fragment transfer is likely but not shown.
7. Two stat cards after men lost 16.5 kg in 8 weeks on a medically supervised diet: sperm concentration ×1.5, total sperm count ×1.4. Andersen et al., Hum Reprod 2022.
8. Two stat cards for folic acid plus zinc for 6 months in 2,370 couples: 34% live births with supplement vs 35% with placebo. Schisterman et al., JAMA 2020.
9. Checklist titled "Before trying for a baby": start 2–3 months ahead, watch weight and waist, improve diet quality, train strength and cardio, no smoking, less alcohol, sleep, see a doctor for fertility worries.
